Questions

Frequently asked

Need help after purchase? Every buyer gets support. If you need help with activation, installation, or finding your access email, contact support@getinvokitpro.com.
Does it really work without internet?
Yes, completely. InvoKit Pro stores all your data locally on your device using IndexedDB. Once installed, it works with no internet connection at all — creating invoices, viewing clients, exporting PDFs, everything.
What happens to my data if I lose my device?
Use the built-in Backup feature (Settings → Data & Export → Backup All Data) to export a JSON file. Save it to Google Drive, Dropbox or a USB drive. You can restore it on any device with your licence key.
How many activations do I get?
Annual plan includes 2 activations. Lifetime licence includes 5 activations. Because the app is installed from a browser, we recommend installing it once and opening it from the app icon each time for the most consistent experience.
What is a PWA — do I need to install anything?
A PWA (Progressive Web App) installs directly from your browser — no app store needed. Visit app.getinvokitpro.com in Chrome or Edge, click "Install App" or "Add to Home Screen", then keep opening it from the installed app icon for the best experience.
I am in Nigeria — can I pay in Naira?
Yes. Nigerian buyers can purchase through Selar and pay in Naira. Annual plan is ₦19,900 for 12 months access, while Lifetime is ₦59,900 as a one-time payment.
How soon do I receive my activation key?
Payment confirmation is immediate. Your activation key is sent by email within 24 hours. If it does not arrive, contact support@getinvokitpro.com and we will help quickly.
Do I get updates after I buy?
Annual plan includes all updates for 12 months. Lifetime licence includes all updates forever — including the upcoming modules like Inventory, Expense Tracking, POS and Simple Accounting.
Can I get a refund?
Yes. If you are not satisfied within 14 days of purchase, email support@getinvokitpro.com and we will refund you — no questions asked.
